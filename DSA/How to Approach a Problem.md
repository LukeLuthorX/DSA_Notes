Translating that advice for LeetCode can help you approach problems effectively while maximizing your learning. Here’s how you can adapt the guidance:

### General Approach
- **Attempt the Problem**: Start by reading the problem statement carefully and make several attempts to solve it on your own. Aim to explore different approaches and strategies.

### Timing for Reading Solutions
- **Easy Problems**: If you’re stuck for about 10-15 minutes without making meaningful progress, it’s okay to look at the solution. Before doing so, ensure you’ve tried various methods to solve the problem.
  
- **Medium Problems**: If you find yourself stuck for 20-30 minutes, consider reading the solution. Again, make sure you’ve exhausted your ideas and approaches.

- **Hard Problems**: If you’re struggling for 40-60 minutes, it’s reasonable to consult the solution. At this level, it’s crucial to understand the underlying concepts that may be challenging.

### How to Read Solutions
- **Step-by-Step Approach**: When you decide to read a solution, do not read it all at once. Instead, break it down into smaller parts. Read a section, try to understand it, and see how it applies to the problem you’re working on.

- **Avoid Immediate Code Reading**: Resist the urge to jump straight to the code. Focus first on the explanation of the approach and the logic behind it. This will help you grasp the concepts better.

- **Implement Yourself**: After understanding the solution, go back to the problem and try to implement the solution on your own. This reinforces your learning and helps you internalize the concepts.

### Last Resort
- **Full Solution as Last Resort**: Viewing the complete solution or its code should be a last resort. If you find yourself still stuck after breaking down the solution, then it’s acceptable to look at the entire code to understand the complete picture.

By following this adapted approach, you can enhance your problem-solving skills on LeetCode while ensuring that you learn effectively from each challenge.
